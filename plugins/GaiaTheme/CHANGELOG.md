# Changelog

Every version of this plugin, newest first. The version is in `plugin.json`; it ships with GaiaCP.

## [1.3.1] - 2026-10-26

- Translated into German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese and Turkish (machine translated)

## [1.3.0] - 2026-10-19

- Styles the client area, the public order page (/cart) and sign up too, when Settings → Client area picks this theme

## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-17

- The dashboard's figures and card titles in Literata

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-17

- The admin's card (initials, name, menu) at the foot of the sidebar instead of the avatar in the top bar
- Tighter sidebar groups, filled count badges, a rounded menu drawer on phones
- A chosen toggle button (e.g. Stripe's Live) in the theme's dark teal; a softer green for paid / active

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-17

- GaiaCP's own look for the admin panel, light and dark: neutral greys, the logo's teal, rounded cards, Commissioner and Literata fonts, quiet motion
