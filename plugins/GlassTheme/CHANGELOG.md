# Changelog

Every version of this plugin, newest first. The version is in `plugin.json`; it ships with GaiaCP.

## [1.2.2] - 2026-11-04

- Readable colours (WCAG AA): links, tabs and selected buttons in the logo's own teal instead of a bright one (3.3:1,
  now 4.5:1 or more), and secondary grey text (breadcrumbs, tabs, hints, footer) a little darker

## [1.2.1] - 2026-11-03

- The bell's and the cart's count in the top bar are solid again, so the number can be read

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-03

- The top bar is clear at the top of the page and takes its frosted background only once the page scrolls

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-03

- The top bar is frosted and mostly opaque, so the page scrolling under it no longer shows through; its colour and blur fade out softly below it
- Thin, soft scrollbars instead of the grey bar with arrows (the sidebar, the page)
- Past due / open balance boxes in a clear red, the Watch box in amber, each with a soft edge in its colour; neutral boxes get a fine edge too
- Uptime squares in a softer mint; red and amber stay strong
- Badges as tinted glass chips, and the quick filter chips (Orders, Services) as glass pills
- Tabs that wrap onto two lines (a client's page) keep corners that match the pills inside

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-03

- First version: frosted glass cards, sidebar, top bar, menus, windows and buttons over a soft background in the logo's teal and greens, Manrope text, pill buttons, in light and dark; solid cards where the browser can't blur or the system asks for less transparency
