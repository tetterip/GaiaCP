# Changelog

Every version of this plugin, newest first. The version is in `plugin.json`; it ships with GaiaCP.

## [1.0.3] - 2026-10-30

- The check is as wide as the form it is on (login, sign up, forgot password, domain search)

## [1.0.2] - 2026-10-26

- Translated into German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese and Turkish (machine translated)

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-24

- Its settings page is under Settings → Client area (Settings regrouped)

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-11

- Cloudflare Turnstile on the login, sign up and forgot password forms Settings → General picks; Settings → Cloudflare Turnstile for its keys
