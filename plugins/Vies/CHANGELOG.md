# Changelog

Every version of this plugin, newest first. The version is in `plugin.json`; it ships with GaiaCP.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-26

- Translated into German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese and Turkish (machine translated)

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-25

- Check a client's EU VAT number in VIES from the client form (Check in VIES next to the VAT number), filling the company name when it is empty; each check is kept
- Settings → VIES VAT check: your EU country and VAT number (sent with each check so VIES returns a consultation number), and the reverse charge: no tax on the payment notices of EU businesses in another EU country whose VAT number VIES found valid, with a note printed on the notice
