# Changelog

Every version of this plugin, newest first. The version is in `plugin.json`; it ships with GaiaCP.

## [1.0.3] - 2026-10-26

- Translated into German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese and Turkish (machine translated)

## [1.0.2] - 2026-10-24

- Its settings page is under Settings → Billing (Settings regrouped)

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-12

- Settings → Bank transfer now sits under Settings → Payment methods, in its list and in the menu, in place of its own card
- An On switch at the top of Settings → Bank transfer turns the plugin on or off (the same as Settings → Plugins), and the
  page stays open while it is off

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-11

- Bank transfer as a payment method, with the payment instructions (bank details) printed on payment notices and
  their emails, edited under Settings → Bank transfer
