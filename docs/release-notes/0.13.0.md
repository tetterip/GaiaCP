See your servers and hosting accounts at a glance, change hosting plans easily, and keep track of refunds, chargebacks and fees from Stripe and PayPal.

**New**
- **Server CPU.** The dashboard's Servers card shows each server's CPU over 24 hours, next to its accounts and uptime. The bell rings when a server stays busy.
- **On the server.** A hosting service shows whether its account is active, suspended or missing on the server, with a one-click fix when it differs. **Log in to cPanel / Plesk** opens the client's account.
- **Changing a hosting plan.** Another product changes the label and package too, and a new package is set on the server. Package lists show disk space and bandwidth; cPanel reseller prefixes are added for you.
- **Refunds and chargebacks.** Refunds and chargebacks made at Stripe or PayPal ring the bell and show on the payment. Refund payment can give the money back at Stripe or PayPal.
- **Provider fees.** What Stripe and PayPal keep from each payment is stored and shown in Payments and Annual income.
- **Other ways to pay.** A new plugin for cheques, cash, IRIS, Revolut and more, with instructions on payment notices.
- **Emails tab** on the client page, **typed dates** (`9.4.28`) and **copyable text** in clickable table rows.

**Security**
- Dependencies updated for new advisories (league/csv, Symfony routing, http-kernel, mailer, html-sanitizer). No issues found in the audit of this release's changes.

**Fixed**
- Account → Change package always reaches the server (it skipped it when the service already named the package).
- AI domain name suggestions no longer fail with "No suggestions".

**Before you update**
- Stripe and PayPal: tick the new webhook events listed on their settings pages, to get refunds and chargebacks.
- cPanel, DirectAdmin and HestiaCP servers: fill in **Processor cores** on each server to see its CPU.
