A fix for domain renewals on payment, plus a few small improvements.

**Fixed**
- **No double domain renewals.** Recording a payment on a domain now checks its expiry at the registrar first. A domain already renewed (by hand, or imported from WHMCS with an old expiry date) is not renewed again. If the expiry can't be read, nothing is renewed and the renewal waits in Services → Module queue.
- The plugin catalogue's update button reads **Update to <version>**, which is clearer in every language.
- WHMCS import: client login problems show in the admin's language.

**New**
- Settings → Servers and Registrars list the active ones first.
- Help: the Plugins and integrations chapter is rewritten.

**Before you update**
- Nothing to do. If a domain renewal failed after a payment, check its expiry (Sync on the domain) before retrying it in the Module queue.
