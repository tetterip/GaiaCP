# Code of conduct

GaiaCP is built by people who sell hosting and domains, and by people who write code for them. Everyone is welcome,
whatever their experience, background, language or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a language that isn't their first.
- Disagree about code and ideas, not about people. Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Keep discussions on topic, and accept a maintainer's decision on what goes into GaiaCP.
- Respect privacy: never post someone's personal details, client data, server addresses or credentials, even when
  asking for help with an install. Remove them from logs and screenshots first.

## What isn't accepted

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, sexual attention, threats, and publishing others' private
information. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

## What happens

Report a problem privately to the maintainer (GitHub: `@tetterip`). Reports are handled confidentially. Depending on
what happened, the maintainer may ask for a change, remove comments, or block someone from the project, temporarily or
for good.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
